Per-head tag & weight

Track every carcass on its own line.

A kill isn't one number — it's individual animals. Butcher Core tracks each head separately, so the tag, hanging weight and cut sheet all live together.

Tag number

Label each head as it comes off the rail so the right meat goes to the right customer, every time.

Hanging weight

Record the weight per animal — the basis for yield, pricing and the customer conversation.

Sheet attached

A head is marked complete once it has a weight and an attached cut sheet — no half-finished jobs slipping by.

New

The invoice builds itself from the cut sheet.

Set your per-pound and flat rates once. When a cut sheet picks "hot dogs" or "vacuum seal," Butcher Core drops the matching line onto the invoice automatically — priced off the finished hanging weight. Add sales tax, finalize, and the amounts are locked. No adding-machine tape, no math on the back of the sheet.

  • A rate card by meat type & processing option — per pound or flat fee
  • Picking an option on a cut sheet auto-adds its billing line
  • Per-pound lines price off the real hanging weight; sales tax handled
  • Finalizing snapshots the rate — changing prices later never rewrites an old invoice
  • Status runs draft → finalized → paid, with collected vs. outstanding tracked

Not every dollar comes off a cut sheet. Somebody walks in for a case of bacon, a box of summer sausage, a bundle of ground — ring it up right there. A counter sale gets its own invoice with its own typed lines, takes the next number in the same run as your processing invoices, and lands in the same reports.

  • Counter sales that aren't tied to a cut sheet at all
  • Bill it to a customer, a farmer, or just Walk-in
  • Type your own lines — description, qty, unit price, taxable or not
  • One invoice number run for the shop, cut sheets and counter alike
  • Finalizing freezes the sale, and a completed one can't be casually deleted
  • Counter revenue shows up in reports beside processing, so you see the whole business

Two-way texting

The phone stops ringing off the hook.

Customers text your shop's own number and your whole team answers from one shared inbox. Send "your order's ready," confirm a kill date, or fire off deposit reminders — no personal cell numbers, no missed messages.

  • Your own number — each shop sends from its own Twilio account, nothing shared
  • Shared inbox — the office, not somebody's phone
  • Saved message templates that fill in the customer's animal and sign with your name
  • Send a photo — or a video — of the packaged order or cut sheet
  • Set your open days & hours; after-hours texts get one polite auto-reply
  • Every text tied to the customer and their order

New number texts in? Add them on the spot

An unknown number lands in the inbox — create the customer and connect the thread without leaving the conversation, or link it to somebody you already have.

Internal notes

Leave a note on the thread for the next person on shift. It sits with the conversation and never gets sent to the customer.

Snooze & auto-close

Handled it? Drop the thread off the active inbox until later. Any reply from the customer wakes it straight back up.

Start the work from the text

Book a new kill entry or open a new cut sheet for that customer right from the conversation — the details carry over.

Opt-outs respected

If a customer texts STOP, sending is disabled on that thread and the inbox tells you why — you can still keep internal notes.

Nothing silently fails

Delivery status comes back from the carrier. A text that didn't land is flagged with a Retry button, not lost.

Multi-shop, roles & security

Run one shop or a dozen — each walled off tight.

Butcher Core is multi-tenant from the ground up. Every shop is its own space with its own name, logo, staff and data — no one sees another company's records.

Your shop, your brand

Each company gets its own name and logo across the portal, and its own animals and cut-sheet fields.

Locked-down data

Nothing is readable or writable without a staff login. Row-level security enforces separation in the database itself.

Roles that fit the shop

Admin, Office Manager and a read-only Viewer. Admins own settings and the money view; office staff run the day; a Viewer can look without touching a thing.

Real-time everywhere

Edits sync instantly across the office and the floor — no refresh, no stale copy of the truth.

Works on any device

Desk browser, the tablet by the scale, or a phone — touch drag-and-drop included on iPad and iPhone.

Bring your records

Import your existing customers, farmers, cut sheets and kills from CSV, with header auto-mapping.

And take them back out

Download a full backup of the shop as JSON, or pull CSVs of cut sheets, kills, customers, farmers and cut days.

Your time zone, your hours

Set the shop's time zone once and dates, cut days and after-hours replies all line up with the clock on your wall.

Sign-out you control

Choose how many idle days before a login is signed out — tight for the office tablet, longer for the owner's laptop.

Nothing loads from a CDN

Fonts, icons and libraries are served from the app itself, so a blocked domain or spotty shop connection can't break the portal mid-shift.

Nothing to install

It's a web portal — open a link and sign in. No servers to babysit, backups handled for you.
