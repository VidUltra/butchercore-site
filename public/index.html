Per-head tag & weight

Track every carcass on its own line.

A kill isn't one number — it's individual animals. Butcher Core tracks each head separately, so the tag, hanging weight and cut sheet all live together.

Tag number

Label each head as it comes off the rail so the right meat goes to the right customer, every time.

Hanging weight

Record the weight per animal — the basis for yield, pricing and the customer conversation.

Sheet attached

A head is marked complete once it has a weight and an attached cut sheet — no half-finished jobs slipping by.

New

The invoice builds itself from the cut sheet.

Set your per-pound and flat rates once. When a cut sheet picks "hot dogs" or "vacuum seal," Butcher Core drops the matching line onto the invoice automatically — priced off the finished hanging weight. Add sales tax, finalize, and the amounts are locked. No adding-machine tape, no math on the back of the sheet.

  • A rate card by meat type & processing option — per pound or flat fee
  • Picking an option on a cut sheet auto-adds its billing line
  • Per-pound lines price off the real hanging weight; sales tax handled
  • Finalizing snapshots the rate — changing prices later never rewrites an old invoice
  • Status runs draft → finalized → paid, with collected vs. outstanding tracked

Multi-shop, roles & security

Run one shop or a dozen — each walled off tight.

Butcher Core is multi-tenant from the ground up. Every shop is its own space with its own name, logo, staff and data — no one sees another company's records.

Your shop, your brand

Each company gets its own name and logo across the portal, and its own animals and cut-sheet fields.

Locked-down data

Nothing is readable or writable without a staff login. Row-level security enforces separation in the database itself.

Roles that fit the shop

Admin and office roles decide who can change staff and settings versus who just runs the day.

Real-time everywhere

Edits sync instantly across the office and the floor — no refresh, no stale copy of the truth.

Works on any device
